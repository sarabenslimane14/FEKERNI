What the usability review changed

Waiting time between the medicines

  • One setting, in Karim's hands. Medicines and times, Medicine 2, "After Medicine 1 is confirmed", waiting time in minutes (1 to 600). The app has no medical default. 30 minutes is a sample so the demo can run.
  • The clock starts at Nora's Done. Medicine 2 is not reminded before that time. If Medicine 1 is never confirmed, Medicine 2 never starts and Karim is alerted.
  • Nora sees what to do next. A card says "Wait until 08:31", counts down the minutes and says "Nothing to do now. Your phone will remind you."

Telling the medicines apart

  • Every medicine screen shows a numbered tag ("Medicine 2 of 2"), the name in large type, the type in words (Tablet, Capsule, Cream, Other) and the dosage. Colour and the picture only add to the words.
  • The reminder also shows Karim's instruction and what the medicine is for. Karim cannot save a reminder over 15 words (SRD N.F.R.2 A.C.3), and a live counter shows how many are left.
  • Sample names are First medicine (a tablet) and Second medicine (a cream), and in Darija الدوا الأول and الدوا التاني. Names, dosage, instructions and purpose are what Karim types. Sample text is marked as sample until he saves.

Video and testing

  • The app has no recorder and must not have one (PRD excludes audio and video). The panel above logs the task and ends by itself when Nora taps Done or Help, or Karim acknowledges. There is no minimum length.

For Nora

  • One thing per screen. Done is large and green. Help is outlined in clay. Both carry an icon and a word.
  • Reading text from 25 px up (SRD minimum 18 pt, about 24 px). Buttons are 86 to 104 px tall.
  • The follow-up asks "Have you taken it?" ("used" for a cream) and never tells Nora to take it again.

For Karim

  • Urgency is a word, an icon, a filled bar and a thicker border. Alerts name the medicine and its number, so Medicine 1 and 2 are not mixed up.
  • If he does not acknowledge within 2 minutes, a call banner appears and the log records the call.
  • The app cannot give medical advice. It points to Nora or her healthcare professional.

Measured contrast

  • Body text on the screen background 15.7 to 1, secondary text 8.5 to 1
  • White on the green Done button 5.4 to 1, clay Help label on white 6.2 to 1
  • Component borders 4.1 to 1 (WCAG asks for 3 to 1)

Notes for building

  • The clock is simulated. Timing follows the SRD: follow-ups at 30 s and every 30 s, urgent alert at 3:00, Help alert at once, call after 2 minutes. 1x is real time. Use 1x for sessions.
  • The caregiver's entries stay in this browser only. A real build stores them with the account and requires an authorised person to verify them.
  • The third language is Moroccan Darija in Arabic script. Have a Moroccan speaker check the words before any session.
